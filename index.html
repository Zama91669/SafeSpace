How SafeSpace works

Clear pathways. Accountable support.

Different users have different needs. SafeSpace keeps the public safety experience separate from restricted organisation tools.

01

People access support

Community members can use the safety tools and browse the public directory. The platform does not automatically contact services or disclose information.

02

Organisations apply

Applicants create an account, verify their email and submit organisation details for review. Registration alone does not grant verified status.

03

Humans oversee action

AI-generated operational plans are drafts only. An authorised person must review the content before any real-world action.
